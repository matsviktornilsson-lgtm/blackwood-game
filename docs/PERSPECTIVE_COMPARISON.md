# Perspective Comparison — Blackwood

This document preserves perspective alternatives. **Current preference: isometric**, not yet a locked technical implementation.

| Perspective | Strengths | Drawbacks | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2D side-view | Simplest visual reading of vertical climbs; potentially cheaper | Harder to reveal deep settlement volume and alternate approaches | Not selected |
| **Isometric** | Strong balance between dramatic exterior cliff, tactical routes and visible interior rooms | Requires careful cutaway/occlusion, camera and path readability | **Preferred** |
| Full 3D | Greatest atmospheric immersion and dramatic camera flexibility | Highest cost, difficult spatial clarity and camera occlusion | Not selected |

## Isometric design experiment
- Giant natural, irregular cliff; deeply inset barred windows and apertures, warm inhabited lights.
- Exposed forestry far below; werewolves emerge from mist and climb distinct rock channels.
- Show a playable cross-section revealing interconnected excavated rooms behind the face.
- Demonstrate front-layer weapons and deep-layer hospital/food/production.
- Test pan, zoom, outlines, fog, visibility, path telegraphs and selection UI.
- Avoid castles, merlons, outer fortress walls, castle turrets and highly linear rock textures.

Existing art/mockups are atmospheric reference only and do not decide engine or construction geometry.

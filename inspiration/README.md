## Visual direction from the early prototypes

- Use an isometric view and a readable mountain cutaway for the early prototypes.
- Show homes and defenses carved into natural rock, with warm interiors overlooking a cold, foggy forest at night.
- Keep the architecture within the cliff. Some reference images include freestanding castles, fortresses, or towers from earlier explorations.
